trajecto: Description back to ToC

Trajecto is a modular ontology for semantic trajectories: GPS-derived movement of people, the points of interest they interact with, the patterns, purposes and contexts of that movement, and the user profiles learned from it. The core module defines the moving person, entities of interest and interactions, and reuses the datAcron trajectory model (Trajectory, TrajectoryPart, RawPosition, MovingObject) and DUL. It imports the other eleven modules and the purpose concept scheme, and is the entry point to the whole ontology.
